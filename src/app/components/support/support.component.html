Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

To create an account, tap the "Register" button on the login screen. Enter your email address, create a strong password, and follow the on-screen instructions to complete your registration.

How do I add a new property?

Log in to your account and navigate to "Add Property" from the main menu. Fill in all the required property details including address, price, amenities, and upload photos. Save your property to make it available.

How do I manage my rentals?

Visit the "My Rentals" section to view all your active and past rentals. Here you can track rental dates, rental prices, and rental history for each property.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we prioritize your security. All personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We use industry-standard security protocols to protect your data.

Can I edit my property details after adding them?

Absolutely! Go to "My Properties" and select the property you want to edit. You can update any details including price, amenities, photos, and availability.

How do I update my profile?

Tap on "My Profile" from the menu to view and edit your account information. You can update your personal details, profile picture, and contact information.

What should I do if I forgot my password?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your registered email address. Follow the instructions sent to your email to reset your password.

Is the app available on Android?

Currently, Krenter is available on iOS. Please check back on the App Store for updates about Android availability in the future.

Troubleshooting

App Won't Load

Check your internet connection and try again. If the issue persists, try restarting the app or reinstalling it from the App Store.

Login Issues

Ensure you're using the correct email and password. If you can't remember your password, use the password reset option on the login screen.

Photos Not Uploading

Check your internet connection and ensure you have enough app permissions. Try uploading a smaller image file or clearing the app cache.

Slow Performance

Free up storage space on your device, close other apps running in the background, and ensure your iOS is up to date. Update Krenter to the latest version.
